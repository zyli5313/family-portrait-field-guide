# Choosing a scene

Once the photos are ready, the scene decides the mood of the portrait. This page helps match the setting to the occasion and the size of the group.

## Match the occasion

- **Holidays**: a cosy Christmas living room, a snowy front yard, or a fireplace with knitted sweaters.
- **Everyday keepsake**: an autumn park, a garden party or a golden-hour beach.
- **Formal**: a classic studio backdrop.
- **Playful**: art styles such as a hand-painted storybook, a 90s yearbook photo, a snow globe or a 3D cartoon family.

The [scene gallery](https://vibepico.com/scenes) shows each of these with an example and a suggested canvas.

## Match the group size

Small groups suit tall portrait canvases; large groups and groups with pets suit wide ones. Pick a scene whose suggested canvas matches your headcount instead of forcing eight people into a tall frame.

## Use a prompt when you want something specific

If none of the scenes is exactly right, start from a written description. The [family photo prompt library](https://vibepico.com/family-photo-prompts) has ready-made descriptions you can adapt, such as a cosy Christmas living room or a snowy front yard.

## Check before sharing

Look at every face and ask whether the person's closest relative would recognise them instantly. If not, replace that person's source photo and generate again. When everyone passes, the portrait is ready to share or print. Start on [VibePico](https://vibepico.com/).
